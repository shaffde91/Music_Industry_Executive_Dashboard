# Music Industry — Executive Overview

## Project Overview

This project is an executive-level Power BI dashboard designed to provide a high-level view of the music industry using album, band, genre, release year, and certified sales data.

The dashboard was designed to help business stakeholders quickly understand sales performance, industry trends, and the distribution of successful bands across genres and time.

## Business Questions

The dashboard was designed to answer questions such as:

- How many bands and albums are represented in the dataset?
- What are the top-performing bands by certified sales?
- Which genres have the highest certified sales?
- How has album production changed over time?
- How have certified sales changed over time?
- How do these metrics change when filtering by genre or release year?

## Dashboard Features

### Executive KPIs

- Total Bands
- Total Albums
- Total Certified Sales
- Average Certified Sales
- Average Albums per Band

### Visualizations

- Top 10 Bands by Certified Sales
- Certified Sales by Genre
- Albums Released by Year
- Certified Sales by Year

### Interactive Filters

- Genre
- Release Year

The dashboard allows users to interact with the slicers and visualizations to explore different segments of the dataset.
