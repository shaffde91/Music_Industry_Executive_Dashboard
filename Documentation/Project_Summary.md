# Project Summary

## Business Objective

The objective of this project was to transform music industry data into an interactive executive dashboard that provides a concise view of album production, certified sales, genre performance, and top-performing bands.

The dashboard was designed from a business stakeholder perspective, prioritizing KPIs, trends, rankings, and interactive filtering.

## Analytical Approach

The project involved:

1. Reviewing the available music industry data
2. Building the Power BI data model
3. Establishing relationships between the Bands and Albums tables
4. Creating reusable DAX measures
5. Developing executive-level KPIs
6. Building visualizations for sales, genre, and time-based analysis
7. Adding interactive Genre and Release Year filters
8. Applying consistent dashboard formatting and design
9. Testing visual interactions and filters

## Key Metrics

The dashboard includes five primary KPIs:

- Total Bands
- Total Albums
- Total Certified Sales
- Average Certified Sales
- Average Albums per Band

## Key Visual Analysis

### Top 10 Bands by Certified Sales

Identifies the bands with the highest certified sales and allows stakeholders to quickly compare top performers.

### Certified Sales by Genre

Shows how certified sales are distributed across music genres.

### Albums Released by Year

Provides a view of album production over time and helps identify periods of higher or lower release activity.

### Certified Sales by Year

Shows how certified sales have changed over time.

## Interactive Analysis

Users can filter the dashboard by:

- Genre
- Release Year

Selecting a band within the Top 10 visualization also allows users to explore how that band's performance affects other dashboard metrics.

## Business Value

The dashboard provides an executive-friendly way to explore music industry performance without requiring users to work directly with the underlying dataset.

It demonstrates the ability to translate raw data into actionable business reporting through data modeling, DAX, KPI development, visualization, and interactive dashboard design.
